Selected concept
01 / The opportunity

Money goals are easier with a buddy.

Saving is often talked about as an individual discipline, even when the goal is shared.

Buddy explores how a group can save together without losing the warmth of a real relationship. The experience makes progress visible, expectations explicit, and encouragement easy.

“A financial product can be precise without feeling cold.”
02 / Experience principles
01

Make the goal tangible

Every shared pot starts with a clear picture of what the group is moving toward.

02

Show the momentum

Progress is a living visual signal, not a buried number at the end of a transaction.

03

Keep trust visible

Contribution history, nudges, and group updates make the social contract feel safe.

03 / The interface

Clarity with a little personality.

Buddy combines clean financial hierarchy with friendly, glassy surfaces and soft color fields. The result feels useful at a glance and inviting enough to come back to.
